HOW TO READ THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read the SKILL column, not RAW. A shorter board is EXPECTED to score
higher for two reasons that are not skill: it drops the model's own
least-confident calls, and it usually holds less HR — the hardest bar
on the site. MIX BASE prices the second one out; SKILL is what is left.
The findings are:
  1. how big SKILL is, against an interval that says whether it is real;
  2. how fast it decays as the board grows — that is where the honest
     cut-off lives;
  3. whether the top of the board is one market wearing a disguise (the
     lane mix above), in which case a 'precision' board is really a
     base-hit board and should be called one.

This measures the bot's OWN picks. It cannot see the hitters it never
designated, so it cannot tell you a better four existed among them —
that is the prediction_log join in bots/slate_eval.py, a separate study.
