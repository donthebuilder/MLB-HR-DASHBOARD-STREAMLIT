⚡ HRW 60–69 = playable HR timing
🌤️ HRW 50–59 = borderline/building timing
🧊 HRW under 50 = weak HR timing

⛔ = hard score suppression in effect (e.g. True Avoid HR -- score actively capped, not just flagged)
